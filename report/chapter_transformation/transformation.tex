\section{Role in the Kinematic Chain}

The \texttt{Transformation} sub-model is a \emph{signal-only} block (no power ports) that computes the cumulative homogeneous transformation from the base frame to frame $i$.
It is inserted between consecutive joints to propagate the absolute frame position and orientation.

\section{Equations}

Given the absolute transformation $H_{\text{prev}}$ up to frame $i-1$ and the incremental DH transformation $H_i$ provided by the \texttt{Joint} block, the absolute transformation is updated as:
\begin{equation}
  H_{\text{next}} = H_{\text{prev}} \cdot H_i.
  \label{eq:chain}
\end{equation}
From $H_{\text{next}}$ the absolute position and Euler angles of the joint frame are extracted:
\begin{align}
  p_{\text{abs}} &= H_{\text{next}}[1\!:\!3,\;4], \\
  \phi           &= \text{atan2}(H[3,2],\; H[3,3]), \\
  \theta         &= \text{atan2}(-H[3,1],\; \sqrt{H_{11}^2+H_{21}^2}), \\
  \psi           &= \text{atan2}(H[2,1],\; H[1,1]),
\end{align}
where the angles $(\phi,\theta,\psi)$ are the ZYX Euler angles representing roll, pitch, and yaw.

The signal output \texttt{T\_next} (the $4\times4$ matrix $H_{\text{next}}$) is consumed by:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the \textbf{MSe} element in the \texttt{Link} block to rotate gravity into the body frame;
  \item the \textbf{GravityTerm} block (see \cref{chap:gravity}) to compute joint gravity torques;
  \item the 3-D visualisation sub-system.
\end{itemize}
